## Additional implementation mismatch found

The Plan Ahead long-term scenario UI explains P25/P50/P75 × 12. However, the `how_calculated` value attached to a `long_term_cash_flow` recommendation in `advisor.py` currently says average monthly recorded net × 12. That formula is not the formula used to generate the cautious/typical/strong scenarios. If a recommendation card exposes that calculation, its wording can confuse the two calculations. Relevant code: `advisor.py:1908-1918`; scenario calculation: `advisor.py:1576-1614`; UI explanation: `PlanningInsightsPanel.tsx:798-802`.

## Recommended safe wording for the defense

“FinAI calculates financial evidence from each user’s recorded transactions with rule-based backend logic. For Advisor and Plan Ahead, Gemini or Groq may turn that evidence into explanations and recommendations; if neither AI provider is available, the system can use rule-based guidance. Forecasts and goal paces are estimates based on recorded data, and Plan Ahead requires enough history before it shows long-term scenarios.”
